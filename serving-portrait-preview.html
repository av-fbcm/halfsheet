Aug 2 — communion Sunday
WHO'S SERVING TODAY
First Baptist Muncie — Communion Sunday
August 2, 2026  ·  Promotion Sunday
Presiding: Rev. Kendall Ellis  ·  Reading: Janis Wright  ·  Preaching: Rev. Jonathan Balmer
Deacons Serving
Gayle Songer  ·  Jim Butler  ·  Richard Flaherty
Before Worship
• Floating greeter — the deacon of the week greets and floats before and during the gathering.
• Set the table. Deacons set up; the Deacon Chair prepares the elements. Gloves and a clean cloth.
• Confirm your three roles: two deacons serve the cup, one deacon carries a tray to those who cannot come forward.
• Tray setup — two stacks, lid on top. Each stack: Juice, then Bread, then a third tray. One stack's third tray is empty (for symmetry); the other's is Bread & Juice combined — that is the one taken to people in their seats.
During Worship
• Scripture Reading — Janis Wright. Deacons alternate reading; if it is your week and you cannot, arrange a reader.
• Communion — the Pastors lead: prayers, words of institution, and serving the bread. Two deacons serve the cup. One deacon takes a tray to those unable to process forward.
• Mercy offering — collected by the deacons at the exits on the way out.
After Worship
• Package enough remaining elements for the homebound members in your circle.
• Dispose respectfully of what remains, and clean all supplies.
• Visit within one week. If you cannot, trade with another deacon or schedule another time this month.
